## Unsupported match mechanics

- Penalties, cards (and sendings-off), injuries, own goals, drop balls, advantage play, extra time and shoot-outs are not simulated.
- Fouls never occur inside the penalty area (so no penalties arise).
- No off-ball player positions, formations in motion or dribble/carry events. Carries are implicit: the next action may start a few metres from where the previous pass ended.
- Goalkeepers only act near their own goal: they receive deliberate back passes, take goal kicks, hold saves and claim loose balls; they never join attacks.
- Substitutions occur only in the second half (including at half-time) and like-for-like where possible.
- Shot quality is an internal function of distance and body part; it is not emitted in events.
